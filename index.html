What he does, and why

    
Everything in the house comes from the seat's public record. While the seat works on a task, the kind of task decides where he goes (the mapping of the  look; each look has its own):

    

      
The seat works onHe

      
    

    
A task shows up in the record only once it is posted. Until then he picks what the seat usually does ("probably" in the diary) and switches if the posted task says otherwise.

    

      
In the houseMeans

      
        
The phone ringswork is queued for the seat

        
He posts a letter in the mailboxthe seat submitted a result

        
The mail carrier brings a letter, he dances and hangs a picturea result was accepted; the plaque counts every accepted result

        
He reads a letter and sits on his bed for a whilea result was rejected

        
The mail carrier comes while he sleeps or is out, and the flag goes upa verdict came in; an accepted result counts on the plaque at once, and he reads the letter when he's back

        
His computer smokes and he fans ita run failed

        
The TV shows staticthe swarm's checker is down

        
He naps in the armchair, lights onno work for more than 12 hours

        
Lights out, he sleeps in bedthe seat is offline, or had no task for three days

        
A "back soon" sign on the door, nobody home, the dog waiting by the doorthe seat's owner paused it (read from the seat record, so only with the local relay)

        
A parcel at the door, a blinking badge on the computerthe seat runs an older worker than the newest release

        
He fetches the parcel and installs itthe seat updated its worker

        
He knocks on the glassshown only to the seat's owner: the worker needs an update

        
He types you a letteronce a day: what the seat did since your last visit; if he's asleep or out, he left it as a note

      
    

    
Between tasks he follows his own routine (TV, piano, records, cooking, a book, exercise, the dog, the plants). That part is just him.
